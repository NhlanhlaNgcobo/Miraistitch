# Lead Generation Playbook

Read this when building the offer, capture page, or nurture sequence. Templates are
starting points — always rewrite them in the voice and specifics of the actual business.

## Lead magnets by business type

**Services / agencies / studios** — free audit (SEO, website, brand, security), a
done-for-you template or checklist, a sample strategy/teardown, a pricing/scope guide,
a "see what we built" case-study pack. Bottom-funnel: free consultation or quote.

**SaaS / products** — free trial, freemium tier, interactive demo, ROI/cost calculator,
a template gallery, a benchmark report, early-access waitlist with a founder perk.

**E-commerce** — first-order discount for email, a size/fit or product-selector quiz,
a style/how-to guide, restock/back-in-stock alerts, a loyalty or VIP list.

**Local business** — free quote/estimate, a seasonal checklist, a first-visit offer,
a booking with a reminder sequence.

**Creators / info** — mini-course, cheat sheet, swipe file, template, a free chapter,
a challenge (e.g. 5-day email challenge).

Pick by **intent**: educational magnets fill the top of the funnel (bigger audience,
colder); demo/trial/quote magnets capture people ready to buy (smaller, hotter). A
healthy funnel usually has one of each.

## Landing page / capture copy formula

A high-converting capture section, top to bottom:
1. **Headline** — the single biggest benefit or outcome, specific. ("Get a free 10-point audit of your website's speed and SEO" beats "Improve your website".)
2. **Subhead** — who it's for + what they'll get + how fast. Remove doubt.
3. **3–5 benefit bullets** — outcomes, not features. What changes for them.
4. **Proof** — a testimonial, logo row, result stat, or sample. One strong proof beats three weak ones.
5. **The form** — minimum fields. Email alone for TOFU; email + one qualifying field (company, budget, role, or need) for MOFU/BOFU.
6. **CTA button** — first person + specific value. "Send me the audit", "Start my free trial", "Get my quote" — never "Submit".
7. **Reassurance microcopy** — "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime." right by the button lowers anxiety.

Strip the nav and competing links on a dedicated capture page — every extra choice leaks conversion.

## CTA button text — swap the generic for the specific

| Generic (weak) | Specific (strong) |
|---|---|
| Submit | Send me my free audit |
| Sign up | Start my 14-day free trial |
| Download | Get the checklist |
| Learn more | Show me how it works |
| Contact us | Book my free consultation |
| Subscribe | Join 2,000+ founders getting weekly tips |

## Email nurture sequence (5-touch starter)

Rewrite subject lines and bodies per business. Timing is a default — compress for hot
offers, stretch for considered purchases.

1. **Deliver + welcome** (immediately) — give the promised thing instantly, set expectations for what's coming, one-line reminder of who you are. No pitch yet.
2. **Value / quick win** (day 1–2) — a genuinely useful tip or insight tied to the magnet's topic. Builds trust and open rates.
3. **Proof / story** (day 3–4) — a case study, result, or transformation. Show the outcome a customer got.
4. **Objection handler** (day 5–6) — name the top hesitation (price, time, risk, "will it work for me?") and answer it honestly. Add a risk-reducer (guarantee, free trial, no lock-in).
5. **The ask** (day 7) — clear, single call to action to take the next step (book, buy, reply). Make it easy and specific; add gentle, honest urgency only if real.

For cold/B2B outreach, keep it shorter and more personal: a relevant opener that proves you did homework, one sentence of value, one specific low-friction ask. Never mass-blast; never fake familiarity.

## Qualification: what makes a lead sales-ready

Score or sort on two axes:
- **Fit** — do they match the ICP? (industry, size, role, budget band, location if it matters)
- **Intent** — have they signaled readiness? (requested a demo/quote, visited pricing, opened/clicked the sequence, replied, set a timeline)

A simple tiering that works without fancy tooling:
- **Hot** — good fit + strong intent → route to a human fast (minutes, not days).
- **Warm** — good fit, low intent → keep nurturing with value and proof.
- **Cold / poor fit** — low-touch or disqualify, so time goes to real opportunities.

## Funnel metrics that matter

Track the whole path so you can see where to fix:
- **Traffic / reach** (TOFU) — volume of the right people arriving.
- **Opt-in / conversion rate** (capture) — % of visitors who become leads. The single most leveraged number on a capture page.
- **Lead → qualified rate** — how many leads are actually worth pursuing (a quality check on the TOFU source and offer).
- **Qualified → customer rate** — closing effectiveness.
- **Cost per lead (CPL)** and **cost per acquisition (CPA)** — judged against the value of a customer.
- **Speed to first response** — strongly correlated with conversion; measure and shrink it.

Vanity metrics (impressions, followers, likes) are inputs at best — never report them as results.
